The classroom game, folded into your phone

Remember drawing little soldiers on a notebook page, marking an ink dot and folding the paper so the ink lands on your friend's side? Paper Fold Battle brings that game to life – with a real paper-fold animation, ink that soaks through, and a twist: you only get to see the enemy for three seconds.

Play in Room

Create a room, share the 6-letter code, and battle on two phones – across the table or across the world. No account needed.

Memory battle

Enemy soldiers are shown for 3 seconds, then hidden. Remember where they were – every fold is a blind shot.

Fair-play Peeks

Peek at the hidden enemy for 2 seconds – but it costs your attack. The player who attacks first gets 3 Peeks; the player going second gets 4.

One round, quick battles

Every battle is a single round: the first to wipe out all 5 enemy soldiers wins.

Your rules

The host sets the soldier size and ink-dot size with sliders – your friend sees the changes live.

Play in One Screen

No internet? Pass one phone between two players. A cover screen hides your soldiers while you swap.

How to play

  1. Each player hides 5 stick soldiers on their half of the page.
  2. The enemy army is shown for 3 seconds – memorize it!
  3. On your turn, mark a blue ink dot on your own half.
  4. Tap FOLD. The paper folds and the ink lands on the mirror spot on the enemy half.
  5. Ink on a hidden soldier? HIT! Otherwise – MISS. Take turns until one army is gone.
  6. There is only one round: the first player to eliminate all 5 enemy soldiers wins the battle.

The Peek ability & turn penalty

What it does: reveals the hidden enemy stick figures for 2 seconds.

The cost: using a Peek forfeits your attack turn immediately.

First-player balance (asymmetric Peeks):

  • Player 1 (starts first): receives 3 Peeks.
  • Player 2 (starts second): receives 4 Peeks to compensate for going second.

Strategy tip: Player 2 has an extra chance to scout, but remember: every Peek skips your attack and gives control right back to your opponent!
