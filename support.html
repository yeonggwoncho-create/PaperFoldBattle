Frequently asked questions

How do I play with a friend?

Both of you open the app and tap Play in Room. One player taps Create Room and shares the 6-character code. The other types the code and taps Join Room. When the host taps START BATTLE, the game begins on both phones. Both phones need an internet connection.

"No room with that code" or "That room already has two players"

Check the code (it never uses the letters I or O, or the numbers 0 or 1). Rooms hold exactly two players – if someone else already joined, ask the host to create a new room. Rooms also close when the host leaves.

My friend disconnected during a battle

The game waits about a minute for them to come back and continues where you left off. If they don't return, the battle ends. A stable Wi-Fi or mobile connection helps.

Can I play without internet?

Yes! Play in One Screen (one phone, passed between two players) works fully offline.

What does "Peek" do?

Peek shows you the hidden enemy soldiers again for 2 seconds. Peeking uses up your attack, so the turn passes straight to your opponent. To balance the first-move advantage, the player who attacks first gets 3 Peeks and the player going second gets 4 Peeks. The number on the Peek button shows how many you have left; when it reaches 0 you'll see "No Peeks remaining!".

Why does the game turn sideways?

Battles are played in landscape so the paper is as big as possible. Menus still work in portrait.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep Paper Fold Battle free. A small banner appears on the main menu, and occasionally a full-screen ad appears after a finished match (never during play). You can control ad personalization in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I reset my statistics?

Open Settings in the app. Your statistics are stored only on your device; deleting the app also removes them.

How do I delete my data?

You don't have an account, so there is nothing to sign out of. Deleting the app removes all data on your device. To ask us to delete online data, email us – see the Privacy Policy.
