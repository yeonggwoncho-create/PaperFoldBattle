Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Paper Fold Battle (the "App"), developed by Yeonggwon Cho ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App on iPhone, iPad or Android. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and show ads.

1. Summary

  • No account, email, phone number or real name is required.
  • The offline mode (Play in One Screen) sends no game data to us.
  • Online play uses Google Firebase with an anonymous ID.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your settings (sound, haptics, sizes), the names you type for players, and your game statistics are saved only on your device. We cannot access them. Deleting the App removes them.

3. Online play ("Play in Room")

When you create or join a room, the App uses Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database) to connect you with your friend. This involves:

DataPurpose
An anonymous user ID created by Firebase (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID)Lets the server know which player in the room you are
The nickname you enterShown to the friend in your room
Room code, chosen sizes, soldier positions, attacks, peeks, round scores and connection statusRunning the match on both phones
IP address and basic technical data (processed by Google to provide the service)Connecting and securing the service

Room data is only readable by the two players in that room. It is deleted when the host leaves the room; rooms that are left behind are temporary and are not used for any other purpose. Google processes this data as our service provider – see the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu and, at most every few minutes, a full-screen ad after a finished match. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped), and diagnostic and performance data

On iOS, the App asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. How we use information

Only to: run online matches, keep the service secure and working, show ads that keep the App free, and fix problems. We do not use your information to identify you personally, and we do not sell it.

6. Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase and Google AdMob), with the friend in your room (your nickname and game moves), or when required by law.

7. Children

The App is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use Play in One Screen to play without sending game data online.
  • Limit ad tracking in your device settings (see section 4).
  • Delete the App to remove all data stored on your device and your anonymous ID.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Because online play is anonymous, please include your room code and approximate time when you contact us so we can find the data.

9. Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database security rules allow each player to read only their own room. We keep online room data only as long as needed for the match; advertising data is kept by Google according to its own policies.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
